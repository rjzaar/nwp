## A. Course versions & audiences

*(Glossary entry + short help block. Term: "version / audience".)*

**Same heart, told for your life**

Every course here has one core — the same truths, the same Church teaching, for everyone. What
changes is the *framing*: the stories, examples, and "how to live this" are told differently
depending on who you are.

We call these **audiences**:

- **Youth** — told through a young person's world.
- **Single** — for life lived on your own before God.
- **Married** — examples drawn from married life and family.
- **Religious** — for those in consecrated or religious life.
- **Priest** — for the priestly vocation.

Pick the audience that fits you and the *same* course speaks in your language. You are not getting a
watered-down or a different course — the core is identical for all of us. Only the way in changes.
*(This is why you will not find "the youth course" as a separate thing — there is one course, framed
five ways.)*

*→ glossary: audience, version, core*

## B. Depth levels

*(Glossary entry + short help block. Term: "depth".)*

**Go as deep as you have time for**

The same learning point can be read short or long — you choose. Look for the depth bar at the top of
a lesson and slide it to fit the moment:

- **Short** — a summary. A minute, the heart of it.
- **Standard** — the normal read.
- **Longer** — more explanation and examples.
- **Detailed** — the fuller treatment.
- **Advanced** — for when you want to go all the way in.

Pick "Short" on a busy morning and "Detailed" on a quiet evening — it is the same lesson either way,
just more or less of it. You can tick **"remember my preference"** so it opens at your usual depth
next time. Depth and audience are separate choices: set both to fit *you*.

*→ glossary: depth, audience*

## C. Contribute a story · suggest an edit

*(Help block. Terms: "contribute a story", "suggest an edit". This is the ops#62 home for both P70's
"add your story" and P72's "suggest an edit" — keep them side by side with Help / Feedback /
Report-an-error, and explain when to use each.)*

**Help make this better — two ways**

You are not just a reader here. Two small buttons let you give back:

**1. Share your story.** Read something that reminds you of a moment in your own life — a struggle, a
grace, a way you learned to pray? Add it. Your story may be exactly what helps the next person. When
you add one, it goes to the community: others read and vote on the ones that help most, and the
Pedagogy Guild gives the strongest a gentle check before they appear in the lesson.

> *A note worth keeping:* writing your story helps *you* even if it never gets published — putting
> your own experience into words is itself a spiritual and learning practice. So share freely. The
> act is the gift.

**2. Suggest an edit.** Spot a typo, an unclear sentence, a better example, or a quiz answer that
looks wrong? Use "Suggest an edit" right next to that part of the lesson. Your suggestion is carried
straight to the right guild's review queue — you do not need a separate account or a second login.

**How it is reviewed.** Nothing you suggest goes live on its own. Every suggestion is read by the
**guild** that owns that kind of content — the Pedagogy Guild for teaching and examples, the
**Theology Guild** for anything that touches Church teaching, and so on. They may accept it, ask for
a small change, or explain why it stays as it is. Once approved, your contribution flows out to
*every* place this course is used. Because content here is shared for the good of all *(→ glossary:
CC0)*, it is credited to the community, not signed with your name once it leaves this site.

*→ glossary: contribute a story, suggest an edit, guild, Pedagogy Guild, Theology Guild*

## D. Sojourners → Theology: the apprenticeship

*(Glossary entries + help block. Terms: "Sojourners", "Theology Guild". Grounded in the operator
decision 2026-07-08.)*

**Sojourners — the road we all walk**

If you are a member here, you are a **Sojourner**. There is nothing to join — it is simply the name
for everyone on the way. As you complete courses, you grow through the Sojourner levels
automatically; your completions carry you forward. Sojourners is the **formation** tier: here,
theology begins as **growth in holiness first** — living it — and only then understanding it. That
is the order that matters. You do not have to be a scholar to belong; you have to be walking.

Because you are living these truths, your voice counts. Sojourners are the community who **propose**
how the deepest, unchanging parts of a course are taught — you suggest, from lived experience, and
the Theology Guild confirms.

*→ glossary: Sojourners, formation*

**The Theology Guild — the mentors who confirm**

The **Theology Guild** is the mature tier that grows on top of the Sojourner road. You do not level
into it automatically — you get there by **mentoring and personal approval** from those already in
it. This is the *understanding* tier: the credentialed successor to lived formation, entrusted with
**confirming** the core, doctrinal parts of a course when the community proposes a change.

So the two fit together as one apprenticeship, junior to mentor: **Sojourners propose, Theology
confirms.** Everyone starts as a Sojourner, growing in holiness by walking the courses; some, over
time and with a mentor's blessing, are entrusted with guarding the understanding. Growth in holiness
first — then understanding it.

*→ glossary: Theology Guild, Sojourners, mentor*

## Glossary quick-reference (for `/node/36`)

| Term | One-line definition |
|---|---|
| **Version / audience** | The same core course, framed for who you are — youth, single, married, religious, or priest. |
| **Core** | The unchanging heart of a course — the truths that stay the same for everyone. |
| **Depth** | How much of a lesson you read — short to advanced — your choice, same lesson. |

| **Suggest an edit** | Flag a fix or a better example next to any part of a lesson; it goes to the owning guild to review. |

| **Sojourners** | Everyone on the way; the formation tier, growing by course completion — holiness first. |
| **Theology Guild** | The mentored, mature tier that confirms the core/doctrinal parts of a course. |
| **CC0** | Contributions are shared freely for the good of all, credited to the community, not signed personally once they leave. |
